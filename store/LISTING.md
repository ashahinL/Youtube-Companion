## The package

The same zip goes to both stores: `companion-for-youtube-1.0.0.zip`, 24
entries, 174,899 bytes, SHA-256
`682706219de51def937835587acc098c6926e3cdb935166e8988d1174470e575`.

- Download it from the [v1.0.0 release](https://github.com/ashahinL/Youtube-Companion/releases/tag/v1.0.0), or
- run `npm run pack` on a fresh clone of the `v1.0.0` tag.

The zip is byte-identical across runs **on the same Node build**, not across
machines: the compressed bytes depend on the zlib inside Node. Homebrew's
Node 26.4.0 (system zlib 1.2.12) packs the `v1.0.0` tag to a different hash
than the one above, with the same files inside. To check a zip against a tag,
compare the files, not the hash:

```bash
mkdir /tmp/z && unzip -q companion-for-youtube-1.0.0.zip -d /tmp/z
cd /tmp/z && find . -type f | sed 's|^\./||' | while read f; do
  git -C <repo> show v1.0.0:"$f" | cmp -s - "$f" || echo "differs: $f"; done
```

Checked on 2026-09-14: all 24 files in the uploaded zip match `v1.0.0`.

A later version is packed on its ship day, from its tag, into
`dist/companion-for-youtube-<version>.zip`. The steps are in
[RELEASING.md](../RELEASING.md).

## Images

All in `store/images/`, regenerated by `npm run shots`. **Open every image
before uploading it.** The 1.0.0 commit shipped an empty "A channel at a
glance" frame that was only caught at the Edge upload; an image far smaller
on disk than its siblings is the tell, and `scripts/shots.js` now fails the
run when one is.

`npm run shots` needs a real Chrome or Edge on the machine; Brave's headless
mode will not save a screenshot. Set `CHROME_PATH` if it is installed
somewhere unusual.

**Chrome takes five screenshots, Edge takes six.** The set is ordered so the
first five stand on their own, and Edge gets the channel sheet as its sixth:

1. `screenshot-1-feeds` — Every channel, one feed
2. `screenshot-2-player` — Listen with less data
3. `screenshot-3-groups` — Group your channels
4. `screenshot-4-watchlist` — Add by link or @handle
5. `screenshot-5-sheet` — A channel at a glance
6. `screenshot-6-arabic` — بالعربية أيضًا

Chrome's five are 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 — the Arabic frame earns its place on a
listing that is also published in Arabic.

Upload screenshots **one at a time, in order**, waiting for each to finish. On
Edge, starting the next upload while one was still in flight silently dropped
the earlier file.

## Description — English

Written for 2.0. The stores cap this at 16,000 characters; it is well under.

```text
Keep up with the YouTube channels you care about without signing in to anything, and listen with a fraction of the data.

FEEDS
• Every new video from the channels you follow, in one list, newest first
• A small dot marks what arrived since you last opened the popup
• Put channels in groups and show one group at a time
• Live streams and premieres are tagged; Shorts stay hidden unless you want them
• Filter by title or channel, or show only your favourites

WATCHLIST
• Add a channel by pasting its link or @handle, or press Add while you are on its page
• On a YouTube channel or video you do not follow yet, a Follow button adds it in one press — every channel of a collab video too
• Import your YouTube subscriptions from a Google Takeout file, read on your device
• Star your favourites: they sit at the top and are checked more often
• Mute a channel to keep it in your feed but out of your alerts
• Open any channel to see its latest videos without leaving the popup

ALERTS
• A desktop notification when a channel uploads: one per channel, not one per video
• The toolbar badge counts new videos since you last looked

PLAYER, WITH AUDIO MODE
• Drops the video to 144p and covers it, so you keep the sound and use about 8× less data than 720p
• Switch it off and the video goes back to the quality you were watching
• Seek, skip 10 seconds, change speed and volume from the popup
• A sleep timer: pause after 15, 30 or 60 minutes, even with the popup closed
• Open videos from your feed straight into audio mode
• See how much data you saved this month and all time
• Keyboard shortcuts: Alt+Shift+A for audio mode, Alt+Shift+Y to open the popup

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no Google sign-in, no API key
• Reads only public youtube.com pages, without your YouTube cookies
• YouTube pages cannot tell it is installed
• Your channels, feed and settings stay in your browser: no analytics, no ads, no tracking
• Export your channels and settings to a file, and import them back

In English and Arabic, with a full right-to-left layout.

Free and open source: https://github.com/ashahinL/Youtube-Companion

Companion for YouTube is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC.
```

## Privacy answers — both stores

Chrome Web Store: the item's **Privacy** tab. Edge Add-ons: the **Privacy**
page. The questions are the same; these are the words submitted to Edge on
2026-09-13.

**Single purpose**

```text
A lighter way to use YouTube: follow channels without an account, get a merged feed of their new uploads with alerts, and play YouTube videos in a low-data audio-only mode.
```

**Permission justifications**

| Permission | Justification |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves the user's channel list, recent videos from those channels, settings, and audio-mode listening totals locally on the device. Nothing is sent anywhere. |
| `alarms` | Checks the user's channels for new uploads on the interval they choose in Settings. |
| `notifications` | Shows a desktop notification when a channel the user follows uploads a video. |
| `activeTab` | When the user presses Add with the box empty, reads the URL of the tab they are on so the channel of that page can be added. Used only on that click. |
| `declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess` | YouTube's public endpoints reject requests whose Origin is chrome-extension://. One dynamic rule, limited to www.youtube.com requests initiated by this extension itself (initiatorDomains is the extension's own id), sets their Origin and Referer to https://www.youtube.com. It does not modify any request the user's browsing makes. |
| Host permission `https://www.youtube.com/*` | Reads public channel feeds and public video details from https://www.youtube.com/* (no cookies are sent), and runs the audio-mode content script on YouTube pages to lower quality, cover the video and control playback. |

**Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code. All JavaScript is in the package.

**Data usage:** tick **none** of the data types. The extension collects no
personally identifiable information, health, financial, authentication,
personal communications, location, web history, user activity or website
content — everything it keeps stays on the device.

**Privacy policy URL:** https://github.com/ashahinL/Youtube-Companion/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

Tick all three certifications (not sold to third parties; not used for
unrelated purposes; not used for creditworthiness or lending).

## Description — English

```text
Keep up with the YouTube channels you care about without signing in to anything, and listen with a fraction of the data.

FEEDS
• Every new video from the channels you follow, in one list, newest first
• Live streams and premieres are tagged; Shorts stay hidden unless you want them
• Filter by title or channel, or show only your favourites

WATCHLIST
• Add a channel by pasting its link or @handle, or press Add while you are on its page
• Star your favourites: they sit at the top and are checked more often
• Open any channel to see its latest videos without leaving the popup

ALERTS
• A desktop notification when a channel uploads: one per channel, not one per video
• The toolbar badge counts new videos since you last looked

AUDIO MODE
• Drops the video to 144p and covers it, so you keep the sound and use about 8× less data than 720p
• Switch it off and the video goes back to the quality you were watching
• Control playback from the popup: seek, skip 10 seconds, speed and volume
• Open videos from your feed straight into audio mode
• See how much data you saved this month and all time
• Keyboard shortcut: Alt+Shift+A

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no Google sign-in, no API key
• Reads only public youtube.com pages, without your YouTube cookies
• Your channels, feed and settings stay in your browser: no analytics, no ads, no tracking
• Export your channels and settings to a file, and import them back

In English and Arabic, with a full right-to-left layout.

Free and open source: https://github.com/ashahinL/Youtube-Companion

Companion for YouTube is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC.
```

## Privacy answers — both stores

Chrome Web Store: the item's **Privacy** tab. Edge Add-ons: the **Privacy**
page. The questions are the same; these are the words submitted to Edge on
2026-09-13.

**Single purpose**

```text
A lighter way to use YouTube: follow channels without an account, get a merged feed of their new uploads with alerts, and play YouTube videos in a low-data audio-only mode.
```

**Permission justifications**

| Permission | Justification |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves the user's channel list, recent videos from those channels, settings, and audio-mode listening totals locally on the device. Nothing is sent anywhere. |
| `alarms` | Checks the user's channels for new uploads on the interval they choose in Settings. |
| `notifications` | Shows a desktop notification when a channel the user follows uploads a video. |
| `activeTab` | When the user presses Add with the box empty, reads the URL of the tab they are on so the channel of that page can be added. Used only on that click. |
| `declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess` | YouTube's public endpoints reject requests whose Origin is chrome-extension://. One dynamic rule, limited to www.youtube.com requests initiated by this extension itself (initiatorDomains is the extension's own id), sets their Origin and Referer to https://www.youtube.com. It does not modify any request the user's browsing makes. |
| Host permission `https://www.youtube.com/*` | Reads public channel feeds and public video details from https://www.youtube.com/* (no cookies are sent), and runs the audio-mode content script on YouTube pages to lower quality, cover the video and control playback. |

**Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code. All JavaScript is in the package.

**Data usage:** tick **none** of the data types. The extension collects no
personally identifiable information, health, financial, authentication,
personal communications, location, web history, user activity or website
content — everything it keeps stays on the device.

**Privacy policy URL:** https://github.com/ashahinL/Youtube-Companion/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

Tick all three certifications (not sold to third parties; not used for
unrelated purposes; not used for creditworthiness or lending).

## Edge Add-ons — as submitted

Partner Center → Edge → **Create new extension**, then page by page. Every
page but Store listings ends with **Save & Continue** at the top right.

1. **Packages** — upload the zip. Partner Center reported *Languages in
   package: Arabic, English* and the five permissions plus the host.
2. **Availability** — Visibility **Public**; Markets **all 241**, with
   *Make my extension available in any future market* ticked (the defaults).
3. **Properties** — Category **Entertainment**; Website and Support contact
   detail from [Store fields](#store-fields); mature content unticked.
4. **Privacy** — [Privacy answers](#privacy-answers--both-stores).
5. **Store listings** — one row per package language, both prefilled with the
   name from the manifest.
   - **English**: the English description, `logo-300.png`,
     `promo-440x280.png`, the five screenshots, the seven search terms, then
     **Save draft**. No YouTube video, no large tile.
   - Under each image field of the English page, *Duplicate … for all
     languages* copied the logo, tile and screenshots to Arabic.
   - **Arabic**: the Arabic description and the same seven search terms,
     then **Save draft**.
   - Both rows must read **Complete** before **Publish** lights up.
6. **Publish** opens *Submit your extension*, which does **not** submit yet.
   - *Does a tester need credentials, accounts, or any other info?* —
     answered **Yes, I need to provide … other info for testers**. Choosing
     *No* locks the notes box to "No Certification Notes required", while the
     page warns that submissions without notes may be flagged. The
     [notes](#notes-for-reviewers) themselves say no sign-in is needed.
   - **Publish** on this page sends it to certification. The dashboard then
     shows *In review*, with *Update* and *Cancel submission*.

The developer account behind it: Partner Center → Edge program, **Individual**,
country **Egypt** (both permanent), publisher display name **Ammar Shahin**.

## Chrome Web Store — step by step

Not walked through yet; the dashboard may word things differently. Every
answer is above, so each field maps to a section of this file.

1. **Developer account** — at the
   [Chrome Web Store developer dashboard](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/devconsole),
   sign in with the Google account that should own the listing and pay the
   one-time US$5 registration by card. The account settings ask for a
   publisher name (Edge uses **Ammar Shahin**), a contact email that must be
   verified before anything can publish, and — for the EU — whether you are a
   trader. That last one is the owner's call.
2. **New item** → upload the zip from [The package](#the-package).
3. **Store listing**
   - Description: [English](#description--english). Add Arabic as a
     localized listing with [the Arabic description](#description--arabic).
   - Category: **Lifestyle › Entertainment**. Language: English.
   - Store icon: `icons/icon128.png` (128×128; the artwork already sits inside
     Chrome's 16 px transparent margin).
   - Screenshots: the five 1280×800 files, one at a time, in order.
   - Small promo tile: `promo-440x280.png`. No marquee.
   - Homepage URL and Support URL: [Store fields](#store-fields). Mature
     content: No.
4. **Privacy** — [Privacy answers](#privacy-answers--both-stores): single
   purpose, one justification per permission and for the host permission,
   remote code No, no data types ticked, the three certifications, the privacy
   policy URL.
5. **Distribution** — free, **Public**, all regions.
6. **Test instructions**, if the dashboard offers them — the
   [notes for reviewers](#notes-for-reviewers).
7. **Submit for review.** Chrome can publish as soon as review passes or wait
   for a manual publish; either is fine for 1.0.0.

Afterwards, record the Chrome item ID and the date in
[Where each store stands](#where-each-store-stands).

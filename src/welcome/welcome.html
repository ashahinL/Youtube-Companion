Companion for YouTube

Three steps and you're set. No sign-in, ever.

  1. Bring your channels

    Subscribed to channels on YouTube? Bring the whole list across at once.

    1. Open Google Takeout: takeout.google.com
    2. Deselect everything, then select only YouTube and YouTube Music.
    3. In its list of YouTube data, keep only subscriptions, then create the export.
    4. Download what Google prepares and unzip it. This can take a few minutes.
    5. Pick subscriptions.csv from its subscriptions folder.

    The file is read on this computer. Only the channel ids and names are kept, and nothing is sent to Google or anyone else.

    Or add channels one at a time: open a channel or video on YouTube, open the extension, and press Follow.

  2. Try audio mode

    Open any YouTube video, open the extension and turn on Audio mode. The video drops to 144p behind a cover: the same sound, with about 8× less data than 720p.

  3. Pin the icon

    Click the puzzle piece next to the address bar, then the pin beside Companion for YouTube. The icon shows how many new videos are waiting.

    Pinned. You're all set.

Free and open source, with no ads and no tracking.

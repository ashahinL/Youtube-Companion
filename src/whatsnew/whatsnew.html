What's new in 2.0

Still no sign-in, still no tracking.

  • Up next

    Line up what you want to hear. Add videos from Feeds or from the player, press Play all, and they play one after another in a single tab — with audio mode on or off.

  • Channel groups

    Put a channel in as many named groups as you like from its ⋯ menu, then narrow Feeds to one group with a tap. A group exists for as long as a channel is in it, and there is nothing to set up.

  • Bring your subscriptions across

    Download subscriptions.csv from Google Takeout and pick it on the welcome page. The file is read on your device, nothing signs in, and the channels arrive without a burst of alerts.

And a lot more

  • The Audio tab is now Player, and the popup opens on Feeds unless something is actually playing.
  • A Follow button for the channel you are watching — every channel, on a video made by several.
  • A sleep timer: pause after 15, 30 or 60 minutes. It keeps counting after the popup closes.
  • Mute alerts for one channel and keep its videos in your feed.
  • Undo after removing a channel — it comes back with its star and its place in the list.
  • Open the popup with Alt+Shift+Y.
  • A New tag on videos that arrived since your last visit.
  • Audio mode's cover is a picture from your device, never a web address.
  • Checks step back when YouTube pushes back, so you never meet a captcha on your own YouTube.

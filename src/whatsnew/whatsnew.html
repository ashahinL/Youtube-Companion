What's new in 2.2

Still no sign-in, still no tracking.

  • Up next and YouTube's queue, in step

    On the Player tab, Up next and YouTube's own queue now mirror each other. Add, remove, reorder or clear on either side and the other follows. A video leaves both once it starts playing, and you can drag Up next into any order.

  • Every channel's newest videos

    A channel's sheet always shows its newest videos, even when the rest of your feed has pushed them out. Press See more to load older ones from YouTube.

  • A shorter Settings

    Settings is grouped and shorter. Five options are gone, and each now works one fixed way. At the bottom, one line opens Support, Send feedback and this page.

And a lot more

  • One alert per check: when several channels have new videos, they share a single alert.
  • Send feedback from the bottom of Settings: type a note and press Send. No account and no email needed.
  • After a big import, videos appear in Feeds as they load, with a line saying how far it has got.
  • The popup keeps the same width on every tab.
  • Grey text and error lines are easier to read.
  • Settings' number boxes fit four digits.

You can open this page again from the bottom of Settings.

What's new in 2.1

Still no sign-in, still no tracking.

  • Import from YouTube

    Press Import from YouTube and the extension reads your subscriptions from your signed-in YouTube tab. With several Google accounts signed in, you pick which one. The list is read on your device, nothing leaves it, and the channels arrive without a burst of alerts.

  • Your groups on YouTube

    The group chips from Feeds now also sit on YouTube's own Subscriptions page. Pick one to see only that group's videos there. You can turn this off in Settings.

  • Light or dark

    Pick Light, Dark or System in Settings. The popup, the welcome page and this page all follow your choice.

And a lot more

  • Rename or delete a group from the Groups sheet. Renaming onto a name you already use merges the two.
  • New-video alerts have two buttons: Listen, and Add to Up next.
  • When channels fail their check, a chip on the Watchlist shows just those.
  • A ↗ on a channel's sheet opens that channel on YouTube.
  • With several YouTube tabs open, a ↗ in the Player tab takes you straight to one, even in another window.
  • Hover the toolbar icon to read what its number means.
  • Every error is a full sentence in your language.
  • The Player tab's four statistics cards fit in one row.
  • Clear watchlist offers to export your list first.

You can open this page again from the bottom of Settings.
